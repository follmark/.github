<h3 align="center" dir="rtl">الفُل مارك مش حظ.</h3>
<p align="center">Full marks aren't luck.</p>

<br>

<p align="center" dir="rtl">فُل مارك منصّة تعليم للمدرّسين في مصر.<br>كل مدرّس موقعه باسمه، وفُل مارك بيمضي تحت.</p>
<p align="center">Follmark is the learning platform for teachers in Egypt.<br>Every teacher's site carries their own name. Follmark signs at the bottom.</p>

<br>

<h3 align="center">الفكرة · The idea</h3>

<p align="center">
  <picture>
    <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/idea-dark.svg">

<p align="center" dir="rtl">صح + صح = فُل مارك</p>
<p align="center">Egyptian teachers draw a star on a full-mark paper.<br>Ours is two of their ticks, one turned around, locked together.</p>
